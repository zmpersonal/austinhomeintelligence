Fire & smoke

Austin Fire & Smoke Damage: First 72 Hours

A source-linked recovery checklist for Austin homeowners after a house fire, with emphasis on safety, insurer documentation, smoke/soot contamination, temporary protection and contractor selection.

Updated August 21, 2026Austin / TexasPrimary-source linked
Direct answer

Do not let cleanup begin before safety and documentation are under control.

After emergency responders release the property, the first priorities are safe access, insurer notification, damage documentation, temporary protection, and a clear restoration scope. Smoke and soot can affect areas beyond the visibly burned room.

Safety before property recovery

Do not re-enter until authorities say it is safe. Fire can compromise structure, electrical systems and air quality even when exterior damage looks limited.

Document before aggressive cleanup

Create photos/video of each affected room and contents. Keep a room-by-room inventory and retain receipts for emergency lodging, temporary protection and other loss-related expenses where relevant to the policy.

Separate emergency stabilization from full reconstruction

Board-up, water extraction, odor/soot cleaning, contents work and reconstruction are different scopes. A useful estimate should separate them rather than present one opaque total.

Policy questions

Ask how smoke damage, contents, loss of use and deductible treatment apply to your policy. Coverage is contract-specific, so the site should educate rather than promise a claim outcome.
