Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Austin Home Intelligence collects information you choose to provide, such as a property address, assessment answers, name, email address, and—only when you request professional contact—a phone number and contact preferences.

How we use information

We use submitted information to generate or save assessment results, improve our tools, respond to requests, and—when you explicitly request it—connect your request with a participating Austin-area service provider in the category you selected.

Professional-contact requests

We do not automatically send your assessment to contractors merely because you use a tool. If you explicitly request professional contact, information necessary to fulfill that request may be shared with one participating provider for that service category.

Analytics and technical data

The site may use standard analytics or hosting logs to understand aggregate traffic, browser/device information, referring pages, and tool usage. If analytics are added, this policy should be updated to identify the provider and applicable choices.

Data retention and deletion

Information may be retained as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, maintain records, prevent abuse, and improve the platform. To request deletion or correction, publish a working contact email here before launch.

Important launch note

This is a starter privacy policy for the initial website build and should be reviewed for your final analytics, email/SMS providers, lead-routing partners, payment tools, and actual business entity before production launch.
