Before you approve AC work

Four things to check before you say yes.

1

Repair or replacement?

Don't let system age alone make the decision. Consider repair cost, repair frequency, cooling performance, energy efficiency, remaining warranty and replacement cost together.

2

What's actually included?

A useful quote should make the scope visible: exact equipment model, tonnage/capacity, SEER2, labor, electrical work, duct changes, thermostat, disposal, permits and warranty.

3

Does the work require a permit?

A full system replacement in Austin generally needs a mechanical permit pulled by a licensed contractor. See the full permit guide →

4

Are you solving the actual problem?

Poor cooling can also come from ducts, airflow, insulation, undersized returns, sizing, or thermostat/control issues — not just the equipment itself.

FAQ

Questions Austin homeowners actually ask about AC.

Why is my AC running but not cooling?

Common causes include a dirty filter or coil, low refrigerant, a failing capacitor or motor, blocked airflow, or a thermostat/control issue. Our free AC Risk Check walks through your symptoms to help narrow down what's most likely before you call anyone.

Should I repair or replace an older AC?

Don't let age alone decide it. Weigh repair cost, how often it's needed repairs, cooling performance, efficiency, remaining warranty and replacement cost together — see Before You Approve Work above.

How long does a central AC usually last?

Typically 15–18 years as a general reference range. Actual life varies with equipment, installation, climate, use and maintenance — it's a planning range, not a failure date.

How much does AC replacement cost in Austin?

It depends on tonnage, efficiency (SEER2), ductwork, electrical work and permits — which is why a single average is misleading. We're building a scope-normalized Austin pricing dataset; see the market data section above for current status.

Is $15,000 too much for a new HVAC system?

It depends on what's included — system size and efficiency, ductwork changes, electrical upgrades, permits, thermostat and warranty terms. Ask your contractor to itemize each before comparing quotes.

How much electricity does an AC use in Austin?

It depends on system size, efficiency, your home and how much you run it. We don't yet have verified local electricity-rate data to turn this into a dollar figure — see the calculator above for current status.

Can regular AC maintenance actually save money?

Airflow and maintenance problems can raise energy use for the same cooling output by roughly 15% in commonly cited estimates. That's a scenario, not a guaranteed savings number — actual savings depend on your system's current condition.

Why is upstairs hotter than downstairs?

Common causes include duct balancing, insulation differences, and undersized returns upstairs. Worth describing specifically to a technician rather than assuming it's simply that heat rises.

Do I need duct cleaning if my AC isn't cooling?

Not automatically — duct cleaning solves a specific set of problems (debris, contamination), not general cooling complaints. See our Duct Cleaning Check for a symptom-based answer.

Do I need a permit to replace my AC in Austin?

A full system replacement generally requires a mechanical permit pulled by a licensed contractor. Straightforward like-for-like repairs typically don't. See our Austin HVAC Permit Guide for details.

How do I choose an HVAC company in Austin?

Check licensing, insurance, whether they actually serve your area, permit competency, quote completeness, availability, reputation and warranty terms — see How We Evaluate an Austin HVAC Company above.
