Disclaimer

Austin Home Intelligence provides educational screening, prioritization and maintenance-planning tools. Results are not an onsite home inspection, engineering opinion, environmental or mold assessment, pest identification, arborist opinion, HVAC or plumbing diagnosis, pool leak diagnosis, legal advice or insurance advice.

Risk scores are prioritization signals

A score estimates how much attention a topic may deserve based on the answers and data available. It does not predict that a component will fail, that a hazard exists, or that professional work is required.

Public data can be incomplete

Mapping, weather, permit and geographic sources may be delayed, incomplete, generalized or mismatched to a property. Seasonal risk information describes Austin-area patterns and is not a long-range weather forecast.

Photos and remote review have limits

Photos and satellite imagery can add useful context but cannot establish conditions that require physical access, testing, measurements or licensed professional judgment.

Professional services

If the site offers to connect you with a provider, independently verify that provider's licensing where applicable, insurance, written scope, price, warranty and suitability for the work. Austin Home Intelligence does not guarantee contractor performance or future property condition.
