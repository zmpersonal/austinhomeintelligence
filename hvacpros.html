FAQ

Questions before you request a contractor.

How many companies will contact me?

Only the number you choose: one or up to three. Your request is not sent to an unlimited contractor marketplace.

Is this free?

Yes — free for Austin homeowners, currently, with no obligation. Some participating contractors may pay us for introductions, but that doesn't affect what you pay.

How do you vet HVAC companies?

We look at credentials, local business history, reputation over time, and professionalism. See We Do The Checking Before You Get The Calls above.

Will my information be sent to other contractors?

No. Your request goes only to the number of companies you choose — 1 or up to 3. It is not distributed to a broader marketplace or contractor list.

Do contractors pay Austin Home Intelligence?

Some participating contractors may pay Austin Home Intelligence for introductions. Payment does not improve a company's vetting status.

Do you guarantee the contractor's work?

No. Austin Home Intelligence is a matching and vetting service, not the contractor performing the work. Verify licensing, insurance and warranty terms directly with the company before approving work.

Can I request a second opinion?

Yes. Select "Expensive repair / second opinion" and choose up to three contractors to compare.
