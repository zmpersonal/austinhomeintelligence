A star rating isn't enough

How we check contractors.

This is where we differentiate from a generic directory. For HVAC specifically, Texas provides unusually useful verification signals.

🪪

License & regulatory status

Is the required Texas ACR (HVAC) license current and appropriate for the work — verified against the state, not just a number printed on a website.

📋

Disciplinary history

We check relevant state disciplinary-action records. No data isn't treated as a red flag — we distinguish clearly between verified clean, record found, and not yet checked.

🏗️

Austin work history

Where public permit data allows it, we look at relevant local mechanical-permit activity as evidence the company actually does this work in Austin.

🏢

Business history

How long the company/entity has been operating, using state business records and franchise-tax account status as supporting signals.

⭐

Reputation, with context

Not just a star average — rating, review count, age and trend of reviews, and recurring themes rather than a single number.

🧾

Quote professionalism

Whether quotes we see specify equipment/model, capacity, labor, permits, warranty, exclusions and electrical/duct work.

🛡️

Warranty clarity

Whether the contractor clearly explains parts and labor coverage before you approve anything.

🎯

Service fit

Trade, job type, geography, availability, system type and project size — a great contractor can still be the wrong fit for a specific job.

Why the choice matters

Why you might choose 1 vs. 3.

One vetted match keeps things simple when you need help quickly, don't want several calls, or the job is a smaller repair or service visit. Three vetted matches make sense when replacement is on the table, the repair is expensive, you want a second opinion, or early quotes are far apart. Either way, only the companies you choose ever see your request — and you can change your choice before submitting.

FAQ

Questions before you request a contractor.

Do contractors pay to appear here?

Some participating professionals may pay Austin Home Intelligence for introductions. Payment does not improve a contractor's verification status or editorial evaluation — those come from the criteria on this page, applied the same way to every company.

How do you check HVAC companies?

We look at license and regulatory status, disciplinary history, Austin work/permit history, business longevity, reputation with context, quote professionalism, warranty clarity and service fit. See How We Check Contractors above.

Will my phone number go to more than three companies?

No. You choose 1 or 3 vetted companies before submitting, and only those companies receive your request.

Can I choose just one contractor?

Yes. Choosing one vetted match is a normal option, not a lesser one — it's often the better choice for a smaller repair or when you need help quickly.

Can I request a second opinion?

Yes. Select "Second opinion" as your reason and choose up to three contractors to compare.

Do you guarantee the contractor's work?

No. Austin Home Intelligence is a matching and vetting service, not the contractor performing the work. Verify licensing, insurance and warranty terms directly with the company before approving work.

What happens if nobody is available?

If we can't find a strong match for your job, location or timing, we'll tell you rather than sending your information to whoever is left.
