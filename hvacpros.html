FAQ

Questions before you request a contractor.

Is this really free?

Yes — free for Austin homeowners, currently, with no obligation. Some participating contractors may pay us for introductions, but that doesn't affect what you pay or how a company is vetted.

Do contractors pay Austin Home Intelligence?

Some participating contractors may pay Austin Home Intelligence for introductions. Payment does not improve their vetting status or editorial evaluation — every company is evaluated against the same criteria.

How many HVAC companies will contact me?

Only the number you choose: 1 or up to 3. Never more.

Why would I choose 3?

To compare availability, recommendations, project scope and pricing — especially useful for an expensive repair or a replacement.

Will my information be sent to other contractors?

No. Your request goes only to the number of companies you choose — 1 or up to 3. We don't distribute it to a broader marketplace or contractor list.

How do you vet HVAC companies?

We look at license and regulatory status, business history, reputation over time, relevant local work history, professionalism and warranty clarity. See Why Pre-Vetting Matters above.

Can I use this for emergency AC repair?

Yes. Select "No AC / urgent" and choose 1 vetted company for the simplest, fastest path.

Can I request a second opinion?

Yes. Select "Second opinion" as your reason and choose up to three contractors to compare.

Do you guarantee a contractor's work?

No. Austin Home Intelligence is a matching and vetting service, not the contractor performing the work. Verify licensing, insurance and warranty terms directly with the company before approving work.
